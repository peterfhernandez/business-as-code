Frequently asked questions

How does PayChase connect to my accounting software?

We use secure OAuth connections with QuickBooks, Xero, and FreshBooks. Your data stays private and we only read invoice information.

Will my clients know these emails are automated?

No. All emails are written in a natural, human tone and come from your own email address.

What happens at the final escalation stage?

You can choose to send our professionally written legal notice templates or hand the invoice off to collections.

Can I customize the email sequences?

Yes. Pro and Business plans let you fully customize tone, timing, and messaging for every stage.
