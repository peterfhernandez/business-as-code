Frequently asked questions

Can I import existing OpenAPI specs?
Yes. Paste a URL or upload your YAML/JSON spec and MockAPI Studio creates fully functional mock endpoints instantly.
How realistic is the generated data?
Use built-in rules or write your own JavaScript-style expressions to generate contextually accurate data for every field.
Do mocks stay in sync when the spec changes?
Absolutely. Update your spec and MockAPI Studio automatically versions and updates your hosted mock endpoints.
Can I see what requests were made to my mocks?
Every request is logged with full headers and payloads. Replay any request to debug your frontend integration.
