Frequently asked questions

How does AsyncStandup work with different time zones?

You set preferred standup windows for each engineer. Prompts are delivered automatically at the right local time for every team member.

Can I connect it to Jira or Linear?

Yes. Pro users can link issues directly from Slack updates. Blockers automatically create or update tickets in your project management tool.

What happens if someone doesn't respond?

AsyncStandup sends gentle reminders and surfaces non-responders in the daily digest so managers can follow up without awkward meetings.

Is my team's data private?

All standup data is encrypted and stored securely. We never train models on your company's updates. Enterprise plans include data residency options.
