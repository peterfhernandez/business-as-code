Frequently asked questions

Does NotionSanitizer require access to my entire workspace?

You only grant access to the specific databases you want cleaned. We never access pages outside those databases.

What happens to my data during cleaning?

Every change creates a versioned backup. You can preview all proposed changes before they are applied and roll back anytime.

How often can I run maintenance?

Pro and Team plans support daily scheduled runs. Starter users get one weekly automated scan.

Is my Notion data secure?

We use official Notion OAuth and store nothing except temporary processing data. All backups are encrypted at rest.
