Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the transcription?
MeetSum uses the latest Whisper models fine-tuned on founder and investor conversations, achieving 95%+ accuracy on English calls.
Can I upload recordings from past meetings?
Yes. Upload any Zoom or Google Meet recording from the last 90 days. We also support direct calendar sync.
Do you store my meeting recordings?
Recordings are processed and deleted within 24 hours. Only the generated summaries are stored in your account.
Is my data private?
All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We never train models on your private calls.
